## ⚡ Overview

**ReSeen** is an ultra-secure, offline-first forensic message interceptor crafted specifically for Android users. When someone sends you a message, photo, voice note, or sticker on WhatsApp, Messenger, Telegram, or Instagram and subsequently hits **"Delete for Everyone"**, ReSeen preserves the original content instantaneously in your local encrypted device database.

Unlike conventional recovery apps that scrape your contacts or upload your personal chats to third-party clouds, **ReSeen operates under an uncompromising Zero-Leak Guarantee**:
* 🛡️ **100% On-Device Processing** — Your chats never leave your phone.
* 🚫 **No Cloud Database Scrapes** — No chat analytics or private logs.
* 🔒 **Hardware-Backed Biometric Lock** — Protect your intercepted messages with Fingerprint, Face ID, PIN, or Pattern.

## 🚀 Setup Guide

1. Download the latest `ReSeen-vX.X.apk` from the `v1.0.0/` folder.
2. Open the file on your Android phone and tap **Install**.
3. Follow the 4-step quick initial permission flow:
   - **Step 1:** Allow Notification Alerts (To receive instant deletion notices)
   - **Step 2:** Grant Notification Interception Access (Required to read incoming messages)
   - **Step 3:** Allow Photos & Media Storage (Required to save images before deletion)
   - **Step 4:** Enable 24/7 Background Guard (Disables battery sleep restrictions)
